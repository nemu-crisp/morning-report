Meta Pays Up to $17.1 Billion Over Youth Harm

Washington's attorney general announced a landmark multistate settlement requiring Meta to pay up to $17.1 billion and add teen safety controls on Instagram and Facebook: a two-hour daily cap, midnight-to-6 a.m. blocks, school-hour notification bans, and stronger age checks. It is the largest state consumer-protection deal outside Big Tobacco.
